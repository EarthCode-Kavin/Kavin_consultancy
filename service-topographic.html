Topographic Survey & Field Investigation

Using advanced RTK and DGPS instrumentation, we deliver high-precision topographic data essential for the design and construction of wharfs, jetties, bunds, pipelines, and other coastal and marine structures. Every survey is executed with documented methods and traceable accuracy.

Services We Offer

  • Precision terrain and elevation surveys using RTK and DGPS instrumentation
  • Topographic mapping for infrastructure planning, pipeline corridors, and coastal structures
  • Benchmark establishment and geodetic control network setup
  • Creek, tidal channel, and river cross-section profiling
  • Breakwater alignment surveys and dredge-fill volume estimation
  • Land reclamation feasibility assessment and mangrove habitat delineation
  • Shoreline and beach profile surveys for erosion monitoring
  • Digital terrain model (DTM) and contour map generation from field data
