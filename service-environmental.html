Environmental Assessment & Compliance

We provide comprehensive environmental assessment services — from baseline data collection and laboratory analysis through to regulatory clearance and post-project monitoring. Our work covers EIA/EMP documentation, water-sediment-soil-air quality studies, and full compliance support for statutory authorities.

Services We Offer

  • Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) and Environmental Management Plan (EMP) preparation
  • Comprehensive baseline data acquisition — primary field surveys and secondary research
  • Water quality sampling and laboratory analysis for surface and groundwater
  • Sediment characterization and geochemical profiling
  • Soil quality assessment for contamination and land-use suitability
  • Ambient air quality monitoring and compliance reporting
  • Noise level measurement and environmental noise mapping
  • Risk evaluation, disaster preparedness, and hazardous waste management planning
  • Regulatory presentation support for SEAC, SEIAA, and MoEFCC clearances
  • Post-clearance compliance monitoring and environmental auditing
  • CRZ violation assessment and regularisation filings with SCZMA and MoEFCC
  • Ecological preservation strategies and sustainable development frameworks
