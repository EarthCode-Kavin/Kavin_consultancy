Hydrogeology & Groundwater Consulting

We specialize in groundwater resource evaluation using multi-criteria spatial analysis and geological interpretation. Our hydrogeological assessments support infrastructure planning, environmental clearance, water resource management, and academic research with scientifically defensible results.

Services We Offer

  • Groundwater potential zone mapping using multi-criteria spatial analysis
  • Hydrogeological interpretation for project reports and resource planning
  • Aquifer characterization and recharge zone identification
  • Groundwater baseline inputs for EIA and environmental documentation
  • Well inventory surveys and water table monitoring
  • Geophysical data integration for subsurface water assessment
  • Watershed-level groundwater resource estimation
  • Groundwater vulnerability and contamination risk mapping
