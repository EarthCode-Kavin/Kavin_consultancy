Numerical Modelling & Technical Reporting

We combine advanced numerical simulation with rigorous technical reporting to deliver actionable insights. From hydrodynamic and sediment transport modelling to publication-ready maps and academic collaboration, our outputs are built for submission, presentation, and decision-making.

Services We Offer

  • Hydrodynamic simulation — tidal circulation, current patterns, and storm surge prediction
  • Wave propagation, wave climate analysis, and harbour tranquility modelling
  • Sediment transport, littoral drift quantification, and shoreline evolution forecasting
  • Dispersion and advection-diffusion modelling for effluent and thermal plumes
  • Oil spill trajectory simulation and dredge spoil disposal impact studies
  • Harbour siltation analysis and outfall diffuser port design
  • Air quality dispersion modelling and land evolution analysis
  • Publication-ready maps, datasets, and technical figures for reports and research papers
  • Academic support for MSc/PhD scholars — data processing, interpretation, and methodology guidance
  • End-to-end project collaboration with consultants, engineers, and institutions
