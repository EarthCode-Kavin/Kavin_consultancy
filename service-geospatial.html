Geospatial Mapping & Remote Sensing

We deliver accurate, reproducible geospatial outputs using satellite imagery, spectral analysis, and cloud-based platforms. Every map is built on sound methodology — suitable for EIA documentation, academic research, mining assessments, and infrastructure planning.

Services We Offer

  • Land-use / land-cover (LULC) classification and multi-temporal mapping
  • Spectral index analysis — NDVI, NDWI, LST, BSI for environmental and industrial studies
  • Change-detection studies covering mining impact, urban expansion, and ecological shifts
  • Google Earth Engine (GEE) scripting with automated map generation and data exports
  • Satellite imagery interpretation for EIA documentation, research, and planning
  • Multi-sensor data fusion for comprehensive environmental baseline assessment
  • Temporal trend analysis for vegetation health, surface water, and thermal anomalies
  • Custom GIS map production with publication-ready cartographic output
