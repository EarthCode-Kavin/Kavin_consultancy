Geophysical Instruments

Field-grade instrumentation for subsurface investigation, groundwater exploration, and geological mapping

ADMT-300SX

  • Automatic Detection & Mapping Technology
  • Multi-channel underground water detection up to 300m depth
  • 3D profiling of aquifers and geological strata
  • Ideal for borewell site selection and hydrogeological surveys

3D Ground Locator

  • Three-dimensional subsurface scanning and imaging
  • Detection of water tables, cavities, and mineral deposits
  • Real-time 3D mapping with depth profiling up to 200m+
  • Non-invasive exploration for site investigation and planning

VES Equipment

  • Vertical Electrical Sounding (Schlumberger & Wenner arrays)
  • Resistivity measurement for subsurface layer identification
  • Groundwater table depth and aquifer thickness estimation
  • Suitable for hydrogeological and geotechnical investigations

Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR)

  • High-resolution subsurface imaging using electromagnetic pulses
  • Detection of buried utilities, voids, and geological features
  • Non-destructive testing for construction and infrastructure
  • Stratigraphic profiling and shallow depth investigations

Proton Magnetometer

  • Total magnetic field intensity measurement
  • Mineral exploration and geological structure mapping
  • Detection of buried metallic objects and dyke intrusions
  • Magnetic anomaly surveys for subsurface characterization

Seismograph & Geophones

  • Seismic refraction and reflection surveys
  • Subsurface velocity profiling for depth-to-bedrock estimation
  • Foundation assessment for infrastructure projects
  • Multi-channel geophone arrays for detailed subsurface imaging

Multi-Electrode Resistivity System

  • 2D Electrical Resistivity Tomography (ERT) imaging
  • Automated multi-electrode data acquisition
  • Contamination plume mapping and landfill delineation
  • Lithological boundary detection and fault zone identification

Water Level Indicator & Dip Meter

  • Borehole water level measurement with precision tape
  • Real-time water table depth recording
  • Well monitoring for hydrogeological assessments
  • Compatible with observation wells, piezometers, and open boreholes
